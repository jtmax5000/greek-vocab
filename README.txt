Greek Vocab Drill - offline app

LAPTOP
  Open index.html in any browser. No internet needed. Progress is saved in that browser.

IPHONE (one-time setup, needs internet once)
  1. Upload this whole folder to any free static web host that gives you an https:// address.
  2. Open that address in Safari on the iPhone.
  3. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  After that the app opens from the home screen with no connection.
